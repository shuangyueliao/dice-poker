How to Play

  • 5 dice, 13 rounds. Each turn you may roll up to 3 times — after a roll, tap dice to hold them, then re-roll the rest.
  • Every turn must end by writing the hand into one empty scorecard row. Rows cannot be reused.
  • Upper rows score the sum of matching dice. Total 63+ across the top for a +35 bonus.
  • 3 / 4 of a Kind and Chance score all five dice. Full House 25. Small Straight (four in a row) 30, Large Straight (five in a row) 40.
  • Dice Poker (five of a kind) scores 50. A later Dice Poker in the same game gives a +100 Joker bonus, and you may write 50 into any empty row (simplified Joker rule).
  • Gold-highlighted rows are the best 3 options for your current hand.
  • Your best score is saved on this device.
